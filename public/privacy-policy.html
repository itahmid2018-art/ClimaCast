ClimaCast Privacy Policy

Commitment to transparency, local-first computing, and zero personal data tracking.

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

ClimaCast ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed with a strict privacy-first, local-first philosophy. We believe that checking weather forecasts, air quality indices, and atmospheric radar should never require surrendering your personal identity or tracking your daily routines.

Key Summary: ClimaCast does not require an account, does not collect personal contact details, does not sell or share user data with data brokers, and contains zero third-party advertising SDKs.

2. Location Information & Prominent Disclosure

ClimaCast offers localized weather diagnostics. When you choose to search for weather at your current location:

  • Foreground Location Access: With your explicit runtime permission, ClimaCast accesses your device's approximate (coarse) or precise (fine) GPS coordinates solely while the application is active in the foreground.
  • Purpose: To request current meteorological observations (temperature, precipitation, wind, UV index, air quality) matching your coordinates from public weather APIs (such as Open-Meteo).
  • Zero Background Location: ClimaCast never requests or accesses background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION). When the app is closed or minimized, location access ceases immediately.
  • Manual Alternative: Location permissions are completely optional. Users can freely use ClimaCast by typing city names, ZIP codes, or PIN codes without granting device GPS permission.

3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

ClimaCast only handles technical data strictly required for weather functionality:

  • User-Configured Preferences: Preferred measurement units (Celsius/Fahrenheit, km/h vs. mph, mm vs. inches), selected AQI source, saved favorite cities, and background imagery preferences. These are stored locally on your device in localStorage or local SQLite database.
  • Network Transmissions: Geographic coordinates or searched query strings transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to meteorological API services to retrieve forecasts.

What we NEVER collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user profiles.
  • Financial details, payment tokens, or credit cards.
  • Contact lists, SMS, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA) or cross-app tracking beacons.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver real-time atmospheric data, ClimaCast transmits standard query parameters to the following trusted endpoints:

  • Open-Meteo: Open-source weather forecast API. No user identifiable data is shared.
  • PurpleAir: Real-time hyper-local community air quality sensor data.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Public geocoding service for resolving city names and postal codes.
  • WTTR.in: Terminal-style ASCII meteorological fallback forecast.

5. Security & Data Deletion Rights

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data exchanged between ClimaCast and external weather services is encrypted end-to-end via industry-standard HTTPS.
  • Data Deletion: You have full control over all stored data. At any time, you can clear all cached forecasts, search histories, and saved preferences by visiting Settings > Storage / General and tapping "Clear Stored Locations & Cache", or by clearing app data in Android device settings.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ClimaCast is a general utility weather app and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.
